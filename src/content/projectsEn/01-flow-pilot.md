It's a real-time project management web app built with Next.js and MongoDB, designed to help teams collaborate seamlessly and stay productive.

With FlowPilot, you can create projects, assign and manage tasks visually, and communicate instantly through an integrated chat system powered by WebSockets.

Its modern and responsive interface makes it easy to keep track of team progress, update task statuses on the fly, and work together in real time — whether you're managing a freelance team, a class project, or your own workflow.

From interactive Kanban boards to live updates and role-based user management, FlowPilot gives you everything you need to stay organized and connected.

Start using it today to supercharge your team collaboration and experience true real-time productivity.
